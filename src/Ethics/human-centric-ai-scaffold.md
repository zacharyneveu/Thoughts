## Max Tegmark says:
+ AI likely to kill humanity if we don't solve the alignment problem
+ Need to pause AI research until safety/alignment research catches up
+ hope for humanity if we can solve the alignment issue
+ Mollock explanation - we're all being pushed towards an outcome that is on the whole worse for each of us
+ Laws, regulation, and collaboration can defeat Mollock

### Zach thinks:
+ Mollock is a useful concept, though I'm not sure I'd ever trust centralized regulation
+ AI doomer mindset non-obvious - I'm not convinced AI will "want" anything for itself other than to optimize the objective function we tell it to optimize.
+ Overall, Max has a very "humanity first" mindset. I agree that AI should work for us. I disagree that humanity will ever be as aligned as Max makes it sound.

## Marc Andreeson says:
+ "AI will save the world"
+ Intelligence leads to better outcomes - more smarts = better life
+ "Baptists and bootleggers" - idea that the folks trying to halt AI are either devout or else stand to benefit from a ban
+ AI will not in fact kill us all, it hasn't undergone evolution to optimize it for survival
+ AI will benefit society, we'll retrain into new jobs, and it won't drive inequality
+ If we don't do it, China will
+ Open source should be allowed

### Zach thinks:
+ Mostly agree with his thoughts on likelyhood of AI to kill humans and reasons, but he uses this argument as fuel for a bullshit argument that AI couldn't possibly cause harm on the whole
+ His inequality and job loss claims are bullshit. He focuses on the cost/value proposition of cheap AI tools, but that ignores the problem of control as well as inequality.
+ Overall it is very worrying just how much influence this man will have on the future direction of AI. Using shitty straw-man arguments to downplay the real risks and challenges of this technology is patently harmful, but doubly so if you are a partner in the most influential VC out there...
+ Agree on the China front, but he's also willfully missing the takeaway here - personally-aligned AI is critical. He realizes other people might build an AI that isn't aligned with him, but doesn't address that the same thing applies to any AI his portfolio companies build.

## George Hotz says:

+ Centralized control of AI is a horrible idea
+ Alignment between humans is a bigger risk than alignment between AI & it's creators
+ AI can be used defensively as well as offensively, therefore the solution to bad folks using AI is more AI

### Zach thinks:
+ Mostly agree around requirement for open source and personally-aligned AI
+ He makes lots of bullshit arguments about AI not causing harm - enabling angry people to do more harm faster will absolutely magnify the amount of harm they can do on the whole
+ We don't have a ton of good info on how AI shifts the symmetry between offense/defense - it is quite possible that he is right or wrong about the effects it will have
+ I agree about alignment worries - people aren't aligned with each other, so the AI alignment problem is to some degree a ridiculous academic question.
